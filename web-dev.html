What is Web Development ?

Web development refers to the creation of websites and web applications. It encompasses both the front-end (what the user sees and interacts with) and the back-end (the server, database, and application logic). The front-end utilizes HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, while the back-end employs languages ​​such as PHP, Node.js, and Python, as well as databases like MySQL and MongoDB.

Why Is Web Development Important ?

In today's digital age, an online presence is essential for every business and individual. A well-designed website builds your brand image, helps you reach customers, and provides a platform for selling your products and services. Responsive design ensures that your website looks great on every device, while SEO-friendly development helps you rank higher in Google search results, thereby generating increased traffic and business.
