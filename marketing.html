What is Marketing ?

Marketing entails reaching your target audience with your products or services and motivating them to make a purchase. Digital marketing is a modern form of this practice that utilizes online channels—such as search engines, social media, email, and websites—to enhance brand awareness, generate leads, and boost sales.

Why Is Marketing Important ?

In today's competitive market, simply having a good product is not enough. You must reach your target customers and demonstrate how your product fulfills their needs. Effective marketing enhances your brand visibility, attracts new customers, and fosters strong relationships with existing ones. This enables your business to achieve sustainable growth.
