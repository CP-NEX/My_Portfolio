What is SEO?

Search Engine Optimization is the process of improving your site's organic visibility. It focuses on ranking higher in Google results without paying for every click.

  • Keyword Research
  • Content Optimization
  • Backlink Building
  • Technical Audits

What is PPC?

Pay-Per-Click advertising allows you to buy traffic. You show ads to a targeted audience and pay a fee only when someone clicks on your link for instant reach.

  • Targeted Google Ads
  • Social Media Campaigns
  • ROI Tracking
  • A/B Ad Testing
